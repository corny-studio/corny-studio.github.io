Strategies paper ledger · not real money

You choose which strategy is active: only its P&L counts in your headline numbers. Every other strategy keeps running as a shadow so you can compare fairly, and switching never loses a position. Open positions are marked at the last alert price, not a live quote.

Pause stops a bot opening new paper trades: its alerts still arrive, but only fills that close what it already holds count. If TradingView is holding a trade the bot skipped, the bot waits until TradingView is flat again, so a later exit can never open a wrong position. Resync closes the bot's paper position at the last price and follows TradingView again. This is the paper ledger only: pausing here does not stop the TradingView alert itself.

Checking…

1Make a password

In Cloudflare open your Worker → Settings → Variables and Secrets (Production tab) → add a Secret named BOT_WEBHOOK_SECRET with this exact password, then Deploy. Not stored on this page: if you leave, paste it here again to rebuild the message.

2Build the alert

The message holds your password. Treat it like one: anyone who has it can post fake fills. It also carries ps, TradingView's own position after each fill, so Pause and Resume can stay in step with the strategy.

3Add the alert in TradingView

  1. Put corny-multi-strategy.pine on a chart. Open its Settings and pick a Strategy (EMA VWAP, Breakout, EMA Trend, RSI Mean Reversion, MACD Momentum or Bollinger Bounce), then click Alert.
  2. Condition: that strategy. Paste the message above into Message. The bot name is filled in automatically from the strategy you picked.
  3. Want to compare several? Add the script to the chart again with a different Strategy and make one more alert with the same message. Each shows up here as its own bot, and you pick which one is active above.
  4. Under Notifications tick Webhook URL and paste the URL above. As far as I know, TradingView needs a paid plan and two-factor login turned on for webhooks.
  5. Use Send test fill below to confirm the ledger works. It doesn't test TradingView itself; the first real alert will show in the fill log.

Messages can use action: buy, sell or close. Optional fee (per contract) and pv are supported. This is a paper ledger: it never places a real order.
