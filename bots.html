1Make a password

In Cloudflare open your Worker → Settings → Variables and Secrets (Production tab) → add a Secret named BOT_WEBHOOK_SECRET with this exact password, then Deploy. Not stored on this page: if you leave, paste it here again to rebuild the message.

2Build the alert

The message holds your password. Treat it like one: anyone who has it can post fake fills.

3Add the alert in TradingView

  1. Put your strategy on a chart (corny-multi-strategy.pine works), then click Alert.
  2. Condition: pick your strategy. Paste the message above into Message.
  3. Under Notifications tick Webhook URL and paste the URL above. As far as I know, TradingView needs a paid plan and two-factor login turned on for webhooks.
  4. Use Send test fill below to confirm the ledger works. It doesn't test TradingView itself; the first real alert will show in the fill log.

Messages can use action: buy, sell or close. Optional fee (per contract) and pv are supported. This is a paper ledger: it never places a real order.
